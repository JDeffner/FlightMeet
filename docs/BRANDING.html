01 — Brand Story

A sky is better shared.

Flying alone is a hobby. Flying together is a community. FlightMeet exists so a pilot never has to guess where the good air is, who's going, or whether tomorrow will fly. It turns scattered pilots into a scene — one meet at a time.

The problem

Good days go unflown

Perfect conditions arrive and pilots miss them — no plan, no group, no read on the weather until it's too late.

The idea

One place to gather

Discover flying meets, judge the conditions, commit with a tap, and coordinate with the people who'll be on the hill beside you.

The promise

Take off together

Less logistics, more airtime. FlightMeet handles the finding and planning so pilots can focus on the flying.

We help pilots turn a good forecast into a shared flying day — find it, plan it, fly it.

MissionMake it effortless for pilots to meet, plan, and fly together — safely and in the right air.
VisionEvery soarable day has a meet on it, and no pilot flies alone unless they choose to.
PositionNot a social network, not a weather app — the place where flying plans actually happen.

03 — Who We Serve

Pilots first — every one of them.

The whole product is built around the pilot deciding, in one sitting, whether to fly. Two supporting roles keep the community healthy.

Primary

The Pilot

Beginner to advanced. Wants to find a relevant meet, know if it'll fly, join in a tap, and coordinate. Speak to them like a flying buddy.

Supporting

The Moderator

A trusted pilot who keeps an eye on the roster. Reads the directory; doesn't change it. Speak plainly and respectfully.

Operator

The Administrator

Runs the platform and manages accounts. Needs precise, unambiguous, no-nonsense language — closer to instrument panel than marketing.

04 — Voice & Tone

Talk like a pilot, not a brochure.

One voice, adjusted by altitude. Marketing copy can soar; product copy stays level; admin copy is flight-instrument precise. Across all of them: short, warm, honest.

Principle

Clear over clever

Say the thing. A pilot mid-plan shouldn't decode a pun.

Principle

Warm, not cutesy

Friendly like a hill greeting, never mascot-silly.

Principle

Honest counts

Real numbers, real status. If it's full, say full.

Principle

Active & brief

Verbs first. "Join the meet," not "Participation can be initiated."

Tone by altitude

SurfaceAltitudeSounds like
Landing / marketingSoaring — aspirational, punchy"Take off together."
Meets, chat, join flowLevel — helpful, immediate"You're in. 6 of 10 spots taken."
WeatherLevel — plain, non-alarming"Light winds, clear skies on meet day."
Admin / errorsInstrument — exact, calm"User deactivated. They can no longer sign in."

Do & Don't

Do — a full meet

"This one's full — but there are 4 more meets in the Black Forest this week."

Don't

"ERROR: Maximum participant capacity reached. Action denied."

Do — you joined

"You're in! See you on the hill."

Don't

"Participation request successfully submitted to the system."

Do — weather down

"Can't reach the forecast right now. You can still join — check again in a bit."

Don't

"502 Bad Gateway: upstream weather provider unreachable."

Do — empty chat

"Quiet skies. Say hello and get the plan going."

Don't

"No messages found. Be the first to post!!! 🎉🔥"

Word list

Say: meet, spot, region, join, cancel, spots left, pilot, take off, flying day, conditions.

Avoid: event, venue, RSVP, user (in pilot-facing copy), leverage, seamless, revolutionary, unlock. Use em dashes never — commas and periods instead.

06 — Logo

Two words, one horizon.

The wordmark is a single unit: Flight in Cocoa Ink, Meet in Sunset Orange, set in Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold with tight tracking. The color break is the identity — it reads as ground meeting sky.

FlightMeet
On Paper Cream
Default. The everyday lockup.
FlightMeet
On Cocoa Ink
"Flight" flips to Warm White; "Meet" stays orange.
FlightMeet
On Sunset Orange
Reverse: "Flight" white, "Meet" ink for contrast.

Clearspace & misuse

Rule

Keep the split

The two-color break is mandatory. Never one flat color for the wordmark in brand contexts.

Don't

Don't recolor "Meet"

Meet is always the brand orange (or ink on orange). No greens, blues, gradients.

Don't

Don't restyle

No outlines, drop shadows, condensing, or swapping the typeface. Bricolage only.

Don't

Don't add a space

"FlightMeet" is one word. Never "Flight Meet" or "flight-meet".

Clearspace: keep margin equal to the cap-height of the "F" on all sides. Minimum size: 96px wide on screen so "Meet" stays legible.

09 — Imagery & Iconography

Real sky, real light.

Photography is warm, atmospheric, and human — paragliders at golden hour, wide horizons, pilots on hillsides. Never stocky, never cold-blue, never over-saturated.

Photography

Golden hour

Warm low sun, long light, real landscapes (Black Forest, Alps, Mosel). Overlay a Cocoa-Ink gradient so Warm White text stays legible.

The sun motif

Rising & repeating

The radial sun (Gold Pale → Sun Gold → Sunset) signals the cycle: fly, land, do it again. Use as a hero accent, never as filler.

Icons

Rounded & light

Thin, rounded line icons (Phosphor). Match the friendly geometry; no sharp corners, no filled-heavy weights.

Signature shapes

Pill / capsule (999px radius) is the house shape — buttons, chips, nav island, status. Cards use a generous 22–44px radius. The slightly-rotated ticker (−1.2°) and dashed flight paths add hand-drawn motion. Liquid-glass cards (blur + warm translucency) sit over photography.

10 — Motion

Move like the air.

Animation (GSAP) mimics flight: slow parallax drift, thermal float, an arc that rises. Motion is smooth and eased, never bouncy or abrupt — and always respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Ease

power2–4.out

Things settle in, decelerating like a landing approach. No linear, no harsh springs.

Entrance

Rise & reveal

Content lifts up and fades in on scroll. Hidden state renders pre-paint — no flash.

Ambient

Thermal float

Badges and the sun drift gently and endlessly on a sine ease. Alive, not distracting.

Signature

The rising sun

On scroll, the sun swells then settles into place — the brand's hero moment.

Timing: entrances 0.6–1.2s, parallax scrubbed to scroll, hover lifts 2–5px at 0.3s. Interactivity feels immediate — a pilot's own action (join, filter, send) updates instantly; ambient motion is the slow part.

12 — Brand Across Surfaces

Same sky, every screen.

The brand holds from the landing page to the weather panel to the admin console — the tone shifts by altitude, the visual system never does.

Meets

The heart

Glass cards over golden-hour photos, honest status pills, one orange "Join". Warm and immediate.

Weather

Decision support

Plain forecast tied to the meet's spot. Calm, never alarming; failure is soft and never blocks joining.

Chat

The plan

Quiet, friendly, instant. Messages appear the moment they're sent. Copy nudges gently, never shouts.

Admin

Instrument panel

Mono type, precise labels, no marketing warmth. Clarity and safety over personality — but the same palette.

If it doesn't feel like golden hour on the hill — warm, calm, capable — it isn't FlightMeet yet.
